# Strategy And Drafting

Use this reference when analyzing fit, selecting ATS language, or drafting application materials.

## Strategy First

Write `strategy.md` before tailoring. Include:

- positioning: the honest one- or two-sentence fit angle
- required qualifications: each requirement mapped to evidence or marked unverified
- preferred qualifications: evidence or `not evidenced`
- ATS vocabulary: exact posting phrases that are truthful, plus where they should appear
- content decisions: what to emphasize, trim, or omit and why
- open submission items: fields the documents cannot resolve

If a required qualification is absent from `resume/master_resume.md`, existing job files, and conversation context, ask the user before claiming it. Do not silently turn an unknown into a match.

## Tailored Resume

Draft `tailored_resume.md` from `resume/master_resume.md` using `strategy.md` as the decision record. Preserve the repository's Markdown structure and the candidate's voice. Use exact posting terminology where truthful and natural.

When space is tight, cut redundant or low-signal content before evidence or keywords tied to required qualifications. Do not optimize for one page by deleting essential evidence.

## Other Materials

Add `cover_letter.typ` when the channel requests one or it materially strengthens the application. Draft an email only for email submission or outreach. Both must use the same evidence and positioning as `strategy.md`.

Cover letters should sound specific, grounded, and conversational: warm without gush, confident without puffery, and built around 2-3 relevant experiences rather than summarizing the resume. Avoid generic corporate openings, inflated claims, and stock phrases like `I am excited to apply`, `ideal candidate`, `dynamic team`, `proven track record`, or `leverage my skills`.

Start with substance: a real connection to the work, a relevant piece of experience, or a concise reason the role makes sense. Explain why the selected experience matters for this employer. Do not manufacture personal connection, mission alignment, motivation, metrics, tools, or expertise beyond the evidence. Use Matt's own prior writing and approved materials as the strongest voice source when available.

After drafting, update `application_state.md` with the phase reached, fit angle, required evidence, ATS keywords, changed files, unresolved questions, and next action.
