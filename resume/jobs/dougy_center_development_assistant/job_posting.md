## Overview
Donor database management, gift processing, and acknowledgement letters, plus administrative event support. During Reflection Benefit season, duties shift toward event preparation and data entry.

## Key Responsibilities
- Process gifts and create donor thank-you correspondence
- Manage contact files using GiveSmart software
- Serve as primary donor contact for contribution inquiries
- Track in-kind donations
- Support Porsche Raffle ticket sales
- Provide general office support

## Required
- Three years office experience with Windows proficiency
- Detail-oriented with strong multitasking abilities
- Excellent communicator capable of managing competing priorities
- Proven track record meeting deadlines

## Preferred
- "Mail merge" familiarity
- One to two years nonprofit fundraising experience
- Connection to the organization's mission
